1. System Navigation & Header Controls
Global
Figure 1.1: Global header bar with location stall selector, operational date picker, cloud sync indicator, owner lock, and quick actions.
1.1 Top Header Controls
  • Location Stall Dropdown: Select your active trading platform (e.g. Main Mandi Yard, Wholesale Shed B). Every bill generated will automatically tag this stall for isolated or combined accounting.
  • Operational Date Picker: Changes the operational working date. Allows inspecting past days' transactions, verifying customer accounts on a previous date, or issuing back-dated bills.
  • Cloud Sync Pill: Displays live connection state:
    • Synced: Local device is connected and current with Google Sheets.
    • [N] Queued: Offline bills stored safely on device, waiting for internet connection.
    • Local: Cloud sync URL not configured. Operating purely offline.
    Clicking the pill opens immediate sync queue details and allows manual 1-click flushing.
  • Owner Lock: Toggles between Cashier Mode and Owner Unlocked mode. When cashiers are on duty, sensitive actions (resetting ledger, annual rollover, deleting customers or bills) are locked behind the 4-digit PIN.
  • Language Toggle (English / हिन्दी): Instantly switches all headings, receipts, and table headers between English and Hindi.
  • Theme Toggle (Dark / Light): Switches between dark high-contrast mode (optimized for 4:00 AM auction yards) and daylight mode.
2. High-Speed Counter Billing & Thermal Printing
Core POS
Figure 2.1: Rapid Billing Desk with catalog commodity tiles on the left and active bill docket on the right.
2.1 How to Record a Bill
  1. Open the Billing tab from the top navigation or mobile bottom bar.
  2. Select Buyer / Party:
    • Choose Walk-in Cash Buyer for spot retail sales.
    • Or select a registered customer (e.g. Ramesh Sabzi Bhandar). The customer's past overdue and today's outstanding dues will instantly display in red.
  3. Choose Sale Type:
    • Direct Cash: Buyer pays immediately. The physical cash drawer balance increases.
    • Daily Credit (Khata): Buyer takes goods on credit. Bill amount is added to their ledger balance.
  4. Add Produce to Active Bill:
    • Click any produce tile from the catalog (e.g. Red Tomato, Nashik Red Onion).
    • Enter the measured Weight / Quantity. The line total calculates automatically.
    • Rate auto-fills from the daily auction board, but you can edit the rate inline for bargaining.
    • To add an item not listed in the catalog, click + Custom Produce.
  5. Complete & Print:
    • The docket computes Gross Subtotal, applies automatic Round-off, and calculates Final Payable.
    • Click Complete & Print Bill. The 80mm thermal receipt modal appears automatically.
Figure 2.2: 80mm Thermal Receipt Slip with firm details, customer balance breakdown, print button, and instant WhatsApp dispatch.
2.2 Thermal Printing & WhatsApp Dispatch
  • Thermal Printing: In the receipt modal, click Print Thermal Slip. Optimized for 80mm and 58mm thermal receipt printers with zero margins.
  • WhatsApp Smart Routing: Click Share on WhatsApp:
    • On Desktop PC: Opens web.whatsapp.com directly in your browser.
    • On Mobile Phone: Launches the native WhatsApp application.
    • If the customer does not have a saved phone number, an inline phone number input appears right in the modal so you can send it immediately.
3. Customer Khata Ledger & Debt Recovery
Accounting
Figure 3.1: Customer Ledger displaying 4-part balance engine, filter controls, Clear Demo Data toolbar, and row delete actions.
3.1 Understanding the 4-Part Balance Engine

Zen calculates customer dues dynamically directly from all bills and payments. Every customer row displays:

  • Opening Due: Debt accumulated from previous trading dates minus previous payments.
  • Taken (Period): Goods purchased on credit during the selected period.
  • Repaid (Period): Cash, UPI, or Bank deposits collected from the customer during the period.
  • Current Balance: Current live net payable due from the customer.
Advance Deposits (Jama): If a customer deposits extra cash in advance, the system automatically treats it as advance credit. Future credit purchases automatically draw against this advance deposit first.
Figure 3.2: Customer Statement modal with period balance breakdown, chronological transaction history, payment collection, and customer deletion.
3.2 Receiving a Payment (Vasooli)
  1. Click Receive Payment in the Customers toolbar, or select + Create -> Receive Payment from any screen.
  2. Select the customer name. Their overdue balance appears immediately.
  3. Enter the Amount Paid (₹).
  4. Select payment mode: CASH, UPI / GPay, or Bank NEFT / Cheque.
  5. Click Save & Print Receipt. Customer balance adjusts immediately, drawer cash updates, and repayment receipt is ready for WhatsApp dispatch.
3.3 Deleting Customers & Clearing Demo Data
  • Deleting an Individual Customer: Click the Delete button on any customer row (or inside their statement modal).
    • If the customer has zero balance and zero active bills, they are deleted immediately without requiring a PIN.
    • If the customer has an active balance or transaction history, Zen demands the Owner Security PIN to prevent accidental balance loss.
    • Once deleted, the customer is permanently removed and the auto-seeder will never re-inject them on page reload.
  • Clear Demo Data Button: Located in the Customers toolbar. Allows 1-click removal of mock demo customers while keeping any real parties you added. Prompts for Owner PIN if any active balances exist.
4. Daybook (Roznamcha) & Cash Drawer Audit
Audit
Figure 4.1: Daybook audit register with cash drawer breakdown, transaction filters, duplicate receipt printing, and CSV export.
4.1 Daily Cash Drawer Reconciliation

At the close of market hours, the cashier reconciles the physical cash drawer against the Daybook summary strip:

  • Direct Spot Cash: Cash collected immediately from walk-in cash bills.
  • Khata Debt Recoveries: Cash collected from past debtors paying off accounts.
  • Digital Inflow (UPI): Bank transfers and QR code payments (kept separate from physical cash).
  • Physical Cash In Register: The exact amount of currency notes that must be in your counter drawer:
    Total Drawer Cash = Direct Spot Cash + Repayment Cash Inflow
4.2 Excel & CSV Audit Export
  • Click Export CSV in the Daybook toolbar to export a complete UTF-8 BOM CSV spreadsheet.
  • Contains Bill #, Date, Time, Location Stall, Party Name, Produce breakdown, Subtotal, Round-off, Final Amount, and Cash vs Digital breakdown.
  • Opens seamlessly in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets without font corruption.
5. Produce Inventory & Spoilage/Wastage Tracking
Stock
Figure 5.1: Inventory Board showing total stock valuation, low stock alerts, inward truck arrivals, and spoilage tracking.
5.1 Inward Arrivals (Aamad / Purchase)
  1. When fresh trucks or shipments arrive at your stall, click + Inward Stock in the Inventory tab.
  2. Select the produce item and enter the inward quantity (e.g. 50 crates or 500 kg).
  3. Enter the truck vehicle number, supplier source, and purchase cost price per unit.
  4. Current inventory stock and stock valuation update immediately.
5.2 Logging Spoilage & Wastage (Kharab / Loss)
  1. Perishable vegetables or fruits damaged in transit or wilted by heat must be written off to keep accounting accurate.
  2. Click + Log Wastage in the Inventory tab.
  3. Select the item and enter damaged quantity. Choose reason: Overripe / Rotten, Transit Bruising, or Moisture Damage.
  4. The system deducts the units from active stock and records the financial loss on your Executive Dashboard.
6. Produce Rates Master & Wholesale Pricing Board
Pricing
Figure 6.1: Wholesale Rates Master with category filters, search box, produce addition, and inline rate editing.
6.1 Setting Daily Auction Rates
  1. Click Rates in the top navigation bar before morning trading commences.
  2. Locate the commodity you want to update (e.g. Red Tomato, Nashik Onion).
  3. Click Edit Rate, enter the new wholesale rate (₹/unit), and click Save.

7. Executive Business Dashboard & Risk Metrics
Analytics
Figure 7.1: Executive Business Dashboard with KPI metrics, aging distribution bar, top debtors list, and revenue leaders.
7.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
  • Outstanding Debt: Total active market balance owed to your firm across all customers.
  • Advance Deposits (Jama): Total excess payments deposited by parties available for future drawing.
  • Sales Turnover: Gross sales value across all spot cash and credit bills within the selected date window.
  • Gross & Net Profit: Real-time profit calculated by subtracting commodity cost prices and inventory spoilage loss from final sales revenue.
  • Stock Valuation: Total value of goods sitting in your shop at purchase cost price.
8. Google Sheets Cloud Sync & Bidirectional Transfers
Cloud
Figure 8.1: Google Sheets Cloud tab with Web App URL configuration, Upload Local to Sheet, Download from Sheet, and capacity tracking.
8.1 Connecting Any Google Spreadsheet
  1. Open your spreadsheet in Google Sheets (e.g. test-table or Mandi_Ledger_2026_27).
  2. Click Extensions -> Apps Script in the Google Sheets top menu.
  3. Paste the complete code from google_sheets_sync.js into the editor and save.
  4. Click Deploy -> New deployment. Select type Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  6. Click Deploy, grant Google permissions, and copy the Web App URL (ends with /exec).
  7. Paste this URL in Zen Settings -> Google Sheets & Cloud tab and click Save & Test Connection.
8.2 Upload Local to Sheet vs Download from Sheet
  • Upload Local to Sheet: Takes your local browser records (customers, rates, inventory) and overwrites the Google Sheets database. If your local store is empty, it warns you before truncating the sheet.
  • Download from Sheet: Downloads all records from your Google Sheet (Customers, Rates, Inventory, Settings) and hydrates your local browser storage. Ideal for syncing a clean slate or adopting a pre-filled catalog.
9. Security PIN, System Resets & Recycle Bin
Security
Figure 9.1: Backup & Maintenance tab with Owner Security PIN configuration, Clean Ledger reset, 100% Blank Slate wipe, and system cloning.
9.1 Choosing the Right System Reset
  • Reset to Clean Produce Ledger: Wipes all customers (`customers: []`), active bills, payments, inward batches, wastage, and demo previous year archives (`archivedYears: []`), but keeps standard produce items and wholesale rates.
  • Wipe to 100% Blank Slate: Completely wipes EVERYTHING to 0 (zero produce items, zero inventory, zero customers, zero bills, and zero archives). Perfect for starting an entirely new business (cleaning agency, wholesale trader, etc.) or downloading fresh data from Google Sheets.
  • Load Complete Mandi Test Dataset: Seeds a 20-commodity produce catalog, 10 realistic customer accounts with multi-day credit aging, and previous year historical archives for training.
9.2 Owner Security PIN
  • The default Owner PIN is 1234.
  • You can update your PIN in Settings -> Backup & Maintenance -> Owner Security PIN.
  • Protects database resets, customer deletions with active balances, and Google Sheets cloud configurations.
10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
FAQ
Q: Does Zen work if the internet drops in the mandi?
Yes, 100%. Zen is built with offline-first architecture. All bills, payments, and ledger balances write directly to device storage. Transactions created while offline are queued and automatically uploaded to Google Sheets the moment your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Q: How can multiple phones and laptops connect to the same shop?
All devices connect to your single Google Apps Script Web App URL. When deployed on Vercel (or over local shop Wi-Fi), every cashier opens your link on their phone, and all devices sync their bills to the master Google Sheet.
Q: How do I back up all my data to another computer?
Open Settings -> Backup & Maintenance tab and click Download Full System Image (.json). Transfer this small file via WhatsApp, USB drive, or email to any computer, open Zen, and click Restore System Image (.json) to clone your entire shop state in 1 second.
