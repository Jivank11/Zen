1. System Navigation & Header Controls
Global
1.1 Top Header Controls
  • Location Dropdown: Select your active selling shed or platform (e.g. Main Mandi Yard, Wholesale Shed B). All new bills will automatically tag this stall.
  • Date Selector: Changes the operational working date. Allows inspecting past days' transactions or back-dating bills.
  • Cloud Sync Pill: Displays connection state:
    • Synced: Local device is connected and current with Google Sheets.
    • [N] Queued: Offline bills stored safely on device, waiting for internet connection.
    • Local: Cloud sync URL not configured. Operating purely offline.
    Clicking the pill opens immediate sync queue details and allows manual 1-click flushing.
  • Owner Lock: Toggles between Cashier Mode and Owner Unlocked mode. When cashiers are on duty, sensitive actions (resetting ledger, annual rollover, deleting bills) are locked behind the 4-digit PIN.
  • Language Toggle (English / हिन्दी): Instantly switches all headings, receipts, and table headers between English and Hindi.
  • Theme Toggle (Dark / Light): Switches between dark high-contrast mode (optimized for 4:00 AM auction yards) and daylight mode.
2. High-Speed Counter Billing & Thermal Printing
Core POS
2.1 How to Record a Bill
  1. Open the Billing tab from top navigation or mobile bottom bar.
  2. Select Buyer / Party:
    • Choose Walk-in Cash Buyer for spot retail sales.
    • Or select a registered customer (e.g. Ramesh Sabzi Bhandar). The customer's past overdue and today's outstanding dues will instantly display in red.
  3. Choose Sale Type:
    • Direct Cash: Buyer pays immediately. The cash in drawer increases.
    • Daily Credit (Khata): Buyer takes goods on credit. Bill amount is added to their ledger balance.
  4. Add Produce to Active Bill:
    • Click any produce tile from the catalog (e.g. Red Tomato, Nashik Red Onion).
    • Enter the measured Weight / Quantity. The line total calculates automatically.
    • Rate auto-fills from the daily auction board, but you can edit the rate inline for bargaining.
    • To add an item not listed in the catalog, click + Custom Produce.
  5. Complete & Print:
    • The docket computes Gross Subtotal, applies automatic Round-off, and calculates Final Payable.
    • Click Complete & Print Bill.
    • An 80mm thermal receipt modal appears automatically.
2.2 Printing & WhatsApp Dispatch
  • Thermal Printing: In the receipt modal, click Print Thermal Slip. Optimized for 80mm and 58mm thermal receipt printers with zero margins.
  • WhatsApp Smart Routing: Click Share on WhatsApp:
    • On Desktop PC: Opens web.whatsapp.com directly in your browser.
    • On Mobile Phone: Launches the native WhatsApp application.
    • If the customer does not have a saved phone number, an inline phone number input appears right in the modal so you can send it immediately.
3. Customer Khata Ledger & Debt Recovery
Accounting
3.1 Understanding the 4-Part Balance Engine

Zen calculates customer dues dynamically directly from all bills and payments. Every customer row displays:

  • Past Overdue: Debt accumulated from previous trading dates minus previous payments.
  • Today's Credit: Goods purchased on credit during the active trading day.
  • Today's Repaid: Cash or UPI deposits collected from the customer today.
  • Total Balance: Current live net payable due from the customer.
Advance Deposits (Jama): If a customer deposits extra cash in advance, the system automatically treats it as advance credit. Future credit purchases automatically draw against this advance deposit first.
3.2 Receiving a Payment (Vasooli)
  1. Click Receive Payment in the Customers toolbar, or select + Create -> Receive Payment from any screen.
  2. Select the customer name. Their overdue balance appears immediately.
  3. Enter the Amount Paid (₹).
  4. Select payment mode: CASH, UPI / GPay, or Bank NEFT / Cheque.
  5. Click Save & Print Receipt. Customer balance adjusts immediately, drawer cash updates, and repayment receipt is ready for WhatsApp dispatch.
3.3 Debt Aging Watchlist

Under the Customers tab, check the Debt Aging Watchlist. It categorizes overdue debts into:

  • 0 - 7 Days: Fresh credit (healthy).
  • 8 - 15 Days: Warning threshold (recovery reminder recommended).
  • 16 - 30 Days: High risk overdue.
  • 30+ Days: Critical chronic debt.
4. Daybook (Roznamcha) & Cash Drawer Audit
Audit
4.1 Daily Cash Drawer Reconciliation

At the close of market hours, the cashier reconciles the physical cash drawer against the Daybook summary strip:

  • Direct Spot Cash: Cash collected immediately from walk-in cash bills.
  • Khata Debt Recoveries: Cash collected from past debtors paying off accounts.
  • Digital Inflow (UPI): Bank transfers and QR code payments (kept separate from physical cash).
  • Physical Cash In Register: The exact amount of currency notes that must be in your counter drawer:
    Total Drawer Cash = Direct Spot Cash + Repayment Cash Inflow
4.2 Excel & CSV Audit Export
  • Click Export CSV in the Daybook toolbar to export a complete UTF-8 BOM CSV spreadsheet.
  • Contains Bill #, Date, Time, Location Stall, Party Name, Produce breakdown, Subtotal, Round-off, Final Amount, and Cash vs Digital breakdown.
  • Opens seamlessly in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets without font corruption.
5. Produce Inventory & Spoilage/Wastage Tracking
Stock
5.1 Inward Arrivals (Aamad / Purchase)
  1. When fresh trucks arrive at your stall, click + Inward Stock in the Inventory tab.
  2. Select the produce item and enter the inward quantity (e.g. 50 crates or 500 kg).
  3. Enter the truck vehicle number, supplier source, and purchase cost price per unit.
  4. Current inventory stock updates immediately.
5.2 Logging Spoilage & Wastage (Kharab / Loss)
  1. Perishable vegetables or fruits damaged in transit or wilted by heat must be written off to keep accounting accurate.
  2. Click + Log Wastage in the Inventory tab.
  3. Select the item and enter damaged quantity. Choose reason: Overripe / Rotten, Transit Bruising, or Moisture Damage.
  4. The system deducts the units from active stock and records the financial loss on your Executive Dashboard.
6. Google Sheets Cloud Sync & Annual Rollover
Cloud
6.1 Google Apps Script Backend Setup
  1. Create a Google Sheet at sheets.new named Mandi_Ledger_2026_27.
  2. Go to Extensions -> Apps Script.
  3. Paste the entire contents of google_sheets_sync.js into the editor.
  4. Click Deploy -> New deployment. Select type Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  6. Authorize Google permissions and copy the Web App URL (ends with /exec).
  7. Paste this URL in Zen Settings -> Google Sheets & Cloud tab and click Save & Test Connection.
6.2 1-Click Annual Rollover (Salana Khata Bandi)

When the financial year ends on March 31:

  1. Go to Settings -> Google Sheets & Cloud tab.
  2. Review the Annual Rollover & Customer Migration preview card. It shows total parties to carry forward and outstanding closing dues.
  3. Enter the new fiscal year label (e.g. 2027_28).
  4. Click Execute Annual Rollover & Carry Forward Dues.
  5. The script automatically creates a brand new spreadsheet in your Google Drive named Mandi_Ledger_2027_28, migrates every party, converts their closing debts into new Opening Dues, copies produce rates, and seals the previous year as a read-only historical archive.
7. Security PIN & Recycle Bin (Deleted Records Vault)
Security
7.1 Owner PIN Security
  • The default Owner PIN is 1234.
  • You can update your PIN at any time in Settings -> Backup & Maintenance -> Owner Security PIN.
  • Whenever a cashier attempts to delete a bill, perform database resets, or change cloud configurations, Zen demands this 4-digit PIN before proceeding.
7.2 How the Recycle Bin Works

Transactions deleted from Daybook are never permanently destroyed:

  • When deleted in Daybook, the record moves into the Recycle Bin Vault in Settings.
  • Produce item weights are automatically returned to your inventory stock.
  • Ledger balances adjust immediately so the customer is not overcharged.
  • In Google Sheets, the row status is automatically updated to:
    VOID - Deleted by Owner (YYYY-MM-DD)
7.3 Restoring a Record from Recycle Bin
  1. Open Settings -> Backup & Maintenance tab.
  2. Scroll to the Recycle Bin & Deleted Records Vault card.
  3. Find the deleted bill or payment receipt in the table.
  4. Click Restore.
  5. The bill immediately returns to active Daybook, produce stock is re-deducted, customer debts are restored, and Google Sheets cloud status resets back to ACTIVE.
8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
FAQ
Q: Does Zen work if the internet drops in the mandi?
Yes, 100%. Zen is built with offline-first architecture. All bills, payments, and ledger balances write directly to device storage. Transactions created while offline are queued and automatically uploaded to Google Sheets the moment your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Q: How can multiple phones and laptops connect to the same shop?
All devices connect to your single Google Apps Script Web App URL. When deployed on Vercel (or over local shop Wi-Fi), every cashier opens your link on their phone, and all devices sync their bills to the master Google Sheet.
Q: How do I back up all my data to another computer?
Open Settings -> Backup & Maintenance tab and click Download Full System Image (.json). Transfer this small file via WhatsApp, USB drive, or email to any computer, open Zen, and click Restore System Image (.json) to clone your entire shop state in 1 second.
